Background notes for the teacher

  • Chuseok and the Mid-Autumn Festival share the same lunar date; Vietnam's Tết Trung Thu is the same festival. Dates shift yearly (mid-September to early October).
  • Erntedankfest is usually the first Sunday of October, though some regions celebrate on the last Sunday of September.
  • Brazil's Dia de Ação de Graças is official (1949) but not a public holiday. Liberia's is the first Thursday of November. Puerto Rico follows the U.S. date.
  • The Challenge text for the U.S. mentions the National Day of Mourning; be ready to discuss multiple perspectives on the holiday's history.
  • Other harvest-gratitude festivals you can add for extension: Pongal (Tamil Nadu, India, January), Homowo (Ga people, Ghana, August), Sukkot (Jewish, autumn).
